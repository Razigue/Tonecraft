- (insight) Drive and Amp are adjacent in the chain, so a SINGLE contiguous 4x oversampling window covers both. The drive adds only its waveshaper cost, not a second polyphase resampling chain. This is what makes promoting drive to Must affordable.
- (assumption) v1 ships ONE drive type, not three: the one the default preset uses. PRODUCT.md listed three lazy-loaded pedals; a Must drive must be in the first-interactive bundle, so the other two stay lazy-loaded content for v1.1.

- (assumption) CPU estimate after promotions: neural amp 10-18 percent of a core, drive plus 2-3 sharing the oversampling window, reverb 2, gate limiter meters 1-2. Total 15-25 percent against a 25 percent budget. Essentially zero headroom left.
