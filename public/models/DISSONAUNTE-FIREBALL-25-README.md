The captures are the amplifier alone, taken in full power mode into a 1x12
V30. Settings, as published by the author: lead channel, Gain 6, Bass 5,
Middle 6, Treble 7, Lead Volume 4, Master 2, Presence 4, Mid Boost off; an
Ibanez PTEQ in the loop (cut 250 Hz, boost 400 Hz, 4 kHz and 8 kHz). The
clean boost is a Wampler Euphoria (Tone 6, Bass 0, Volume 8, Gain 1, Open);
the PD boost a Horizon Devices Precision Drive (Attack 2, Vol 6, Bright 6,
Drive 0). Tonecraft plays the three through the Celestion V30 IR, with its
own boost bypassed, and offers them as the British preset's three channels.
